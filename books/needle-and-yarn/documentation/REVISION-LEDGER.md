# Revision Ledger — Revised Six-Chapter Edition

## Structural decision

The earlier story contained a six-chapter core and later material also explored additional chapters/themes. During the 2026 editorial development, the narrative was deliberately consolidated into a stronger six-chapter dramatic arc rather than stretched to ten chapters.

### Final chapter architecture

1. **The Magic Begins** — Yarn is chosen; arrives at Leola's home; meets Needle and the living basket family.
2. **The First Hello** — Needle and Yarn learn each other's rhythm; attraction and trust develop.
3. **Working Together** — their first meaningful creation deepens their relationship and establishes making as giving.
4. **The Break** — Leola receives urgent news that her granddaughter was injured in a bicycle fall; the shock causes an accidental tension event that breaks Needle; Leola takes him with her while going to the hospital; Needle returns repaired; Needle and Yarn acknowledge their love.
5. **The Return** — winter and Christmas; Leola's human family and craft family mirror one another; the granddaughter receives the hat connected to the accident; Micky's craft basket joins Leola's home.
6. **The Pattern Continues** — spring; Micky trains with Leola; skill becomes inheritance; the sunrise scene establishes continuation rather than a closed ending.

## Major revisions from the earlier repository edition

- Expanded Needle and Yarn from symbolic companions into emotionally developed romantic leads.
- Expanded the basket into a recurring living community.
- Added Southern/Mississippi-influenced cadence while avoiding unreadable phonetic caricature.
- Made Leola's hands a recurring visual/narrative bridge between the human and craft worlds.
- Reworked the break so it arises from a believable human shock event rather than an arbitrary knot.
- Connected the granddaughter's recovery, Needle's repair, and the finished hat.
- Expanded Leola's family presence and Christmas gathering.
- Added Micky's learning arc and second basket.
- Changed the ending from closure to generational continuation.
- Preserved the central craft philosophy: patience, mistakes, repair, love, service, mastery, and legacy.

## Locked status

The six-chapter story direction is creator-approved. Publication-source assembly, copyediting, illustration placement, EPUB generation, and PDF generation are separate gates and must not silently rewrite locked story decisions.
