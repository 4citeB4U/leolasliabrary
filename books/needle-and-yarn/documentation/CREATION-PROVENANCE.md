# Creation & Provenance Record

## Work

**Needle & Yarn: A Love Stitched in Time**

## Originating author and creative authority

**Leola (Sister) Lee** originated the book's story, purpose, craft wisdom, emotional philosophy, and the central Needle-and-Yarn relationship. The pre-revision repository edition documents her dedication, foreword, six-chapter story, characters, themes of patience, mistakes, mending, connection, resilience, and generational craft.

The revised edition preserves that originating authorship and expands the narrative rather than representing the story as newly invented by an AI system.

## Creator / design and development contribution

**Leonard Lee**, Leola's son, developed and directed the expanded book experience and the larger Leola's Library concept as a tribute to his mother.

His contribution includes creative direction for:
- the expanded emotional and romantic arc between Needle and Yarn;
- the living craft-basket ensemble and family personalities;
- Leola's hands as the human presence through which Needle and Yarn create;
- the granddaughter accident and Needle's accidental break;
- the hospital/repair and return sequence;
- the Christmas family gathering;
- the use of Leola's family as inspiration for personalities in the craft-basket community;
- Micky learning from Leola;
- the spring/sunrise ending and generational continuation;
- the claymation visual world, interactive library, instructional videos, and digital publication experience;
- preservation of Leola's craft philosophy inside a digital medium.

Leonard's own digital-development craft is intentionally presented as a parallel to Leola's fiber craft: disciplined practice, learning through mistakes, creating for others, passing knowledge forward, and using one's work to increase other people's capabilities.

## Why the book exists

The work is intended to help younger readers understand love as something demonstrated through patience, care, creation, resilience, repair, teaching, service, and what a person chooses to pass forward.

A central principle of the work is that love cannot always be fully expressed in words. It can be placed into something made by hand and given away: a blanket, hat, scarf, lesson, story, skill, or other useful creation.

The story therefore treats craft not merely as production but as a way of communicating value:
- the maker has value;
- the learner has value;
- mistakes do not erase value;
- difficult work can develop skill and character;
- the work itself can carry care into another person's life;
- mastery becomes more meaningful when it is shared rather than hoarded;
- knowledge and love can continue across generations.

This reflects Leola's practice of creating for her family and for people beyond her immediate family and community who can benefit from warmth, care, encouragement, and handmade work.

## AI-assisted editorial development

The 2026 revision was developed through an iterative human-directed editorial collaboration with OpenAI's ChatGPT.

The AI system contributed prose drafting, dialogue expansion, scene construction, continuity suggestions, and editorial restructuring **under repeated human review and direction**.

Leonard Lee supplied substantive story decisions, corrections, family context, scene causality, character behavior, cultural/dialect direction, emotional objectives, rejection of unsuitable drafts, and approval/locking decisions.

Examples of human-directed revisions include:
- requiring Mississippi/Southern cadence rather than neutral dialogue;
- introducing the Toy-Story-like living basket community;
- making Leola a shadow human presence primarily represented through her hands;
- correcting the implausible telephone version of the accident;
- specifying that Leola's daughter brings news of the granddaughter's bicycle accident;
- specifying the accidental Needle break as an immediate shock response;
- specifying Leola taking Needle with her while going to the hospital;
- specifying the Christmas family gathering and family-derived tool personalities;
- specifying Micky's continuing training with Leola;
- specifying the final spring sunrise and generational handoff.

## Human/AI percentage policy

No numeric percentage is asserted in this record at this stage.

A defensible percentage would require a defined metric (for example: final-token provenance, sentence provenance, conceptual provenance, or edit-distance provenance) and versioned source material for each drafting stage. Those are not equivalent measurements.

Accordingly:

- **Originating story/concept authority:** HUMAN — Leola (Sister) Lee.
- **Expanded edition creative direction and acceptance authority:** HUMAN — Leonard Lee.
- **Original manuscript material:** HUMAN-authored/pre-existing repository source.
- **2026 prose expansion and scene drafting:** AI-assisted under human direction and revision.
- **Final editorial acceptance:** HUMAN-directed.
- **Exact token-level human/AI percentage:** UNVERIFIED until measured from preserved versions.

This avoids presenting an invented percentage as evidence.

## Preservation policy

The historical repository book is not to be destroyed or silently overwritten. The revised edition lives in its own canonical eBook workspace so the original and revised lineage remain inspectable.
