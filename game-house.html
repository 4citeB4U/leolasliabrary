FIVE CROCHET GAMES. ONE HOUSE.

Learn through the
work of your hands.

Gameplay is not ready for release. The new games require the reviewed 3D hand rig, hook-and-yarn contact, input controls and lesson assessments to pass together. Earlier placeholder games are not being presented as those finished games.
01 / FOUNDATIONS

Loop Lab

Form the starting loop, chain and basic stitches through hand, hook and yarn movement—not a button that declares a stitch complete.

Curriculum: Getting Started · Basic Stitches
Awaiting validated rig and stitch interaction
02 / CONSISTENCY

Tension Tower

Practice even loop size and compare the fabric produced with different hook and yarn choices.

Curriculum: Tools and Yarn · Gauge
Awaiting fabric-state and tension validation
03 / PATTERN READING

Pattern Quest

Read the pattern, construct its stitches and rows, and identify a missed or extra stitch.

Curriculum: Reading Patterns · Basic Stitches
Awaiting construction and count assessment
04 / SHAPING

Amigurumi Rescue

Work with rounds and stitch markers, then build and assemble a shaped project using the reviewed lesson sequence.

Curriculum: Amigurumi
Awaiting shaping mechanics and lesson-detail review
05 / APPLIED PROJECTS

Yarnfolk Studio

Apply your skills to a finished commission: choose materials, construct the piece, change colors and finish the work.

Curriculum: Colorwork · Finishing Techniques
Awaiting project construction and finishing checks
